# Little Ocean — Juice Bible (prototype)

**Vibe.** A holiday you keep in your pocket: a little coral boat on a painted sea, three blocky crew fishing on
their own, warm harbour light. References: A Short Hike (soft bounce, warm reward), Sable (calm travel, the world is
the UI), Animal Crossing (catch reveal + rarity sparkle), Heartopia (cute hand-drawn map, gentle chimes). Signature
feelings: *lifting, hanging and dropping over a swell*, *the little "!" before a catch*, *shells pouring into the
pocket and settling*, *the hush and chime of finding a place*. It handles like a toy boat, not a car.

**Signature juice.** Sailing (top priority): heave, pitch and roll from the real wave field, a small surf boost down
a wave face, lean into turns, nose-up on throttle, a wooden click per throttle notch, bow spray and a foam wake that
grow with speed; the camera trails softly, leads into turns and widens its FOV with speed; after ~20 s idle it slowly
widens and a crew member hums. Fishing: bobber dip and a "!" over the crew member, a splashy arc onto the deck, a
cheer pose, then a small inked label "Sand sprat · 14 cm" with "+1" flying to the cargo count (readable in under a
second). Cards only for New, Uncommon and UncommonPlus: NEW! ribbon, size, stars; UncommonPlus pauses only the fish's
arc under a flat inked golden beam (the world never slows). Selling: rows slide in, Sell all pours shell-coins along
curves into the Shells counter with rising ticks, the total rolls up and *settles* with a soft plonk and a shine.
Exploring (outranks rare catches): the crew turns and shows "?" toward a horizon glint before you notice it; on
arrival a hush, a ≤1.5 s letterbox push-in (steering stays live), an ink stamp that lands with dust, a chime
arpeggio, and the journal count pops; a region prints its name like a title card. Homecoming with a full hold: a
harbour bell, gulls and warm window light.

**Dial and limits.** Cozy row of the genre table, pushed to its upper edge on discovery and selling only; frequent
events stay small. Pops 0.28–0.4 s with soft overshoot; shake only on big wave slams in The Swells (trauma ≤ 0.35,
roll-dominant), never on UI; one key for all sound (F major pentatonic), soft timbres (marimba, glass, wood). HUD
follows `/clean-ui` (ambient at rest; no speed readout — the wake and pennant show speed). Never: reward the player
for acting on the fishing (no tap-to-boost, no "Perfect!", no reaction to clicking the bobber; P2); no red flashes,
no world slow-motion, no screen-filling particles, no timers or nagging (the hold-full hint shows once per session),
nothing flashing more than 3 times a second.
